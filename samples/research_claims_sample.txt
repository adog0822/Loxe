Climate Change and Renewable Energy - Research Summary

GLOBAL TEMPERATURE TRENDS

Global average temperatures have risen approximately 1.1 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels as of 2023.

The last decade (2014-2023) was the warmest decade on record according to NASA and NOAA data.

Arctic sea ice extent has declined by approximately 13% per decade since satellite measurements began in 1979.

RENEWABLE ENERGY MARKET

Global renewable energy capacity reached 3,372 GW in 2022, with solar and wind accounting for over 90% of new capacity additions.

Solar energy costs have fallen by 89% since 2010, making it the cheapest source of electricity in many regions.

China invested $546 billion in clean energy in 2023, more than all other countries combined.

The United States generated approximately 22% of its electricity from renewable sources in 2023.

ECONOMIC IMPACT

The renewable energy sector employed 13.7 million people globally in 2022, up from 7.3 million in 2012.

Global investment in energy transition technologies reached $1.8 trillion in 2023, a 17% increase from 2022.

Electric vehicle sales surpassed 14 million units globally in 2023, representing approximately 18% of all car sales.

PROJECTIONS

The International Energy Agency projects that renewables will account for 50% of global electricity generation by 2030.

Battery storage capacity is expected to grow 15x between 2023 and 2030, reaching over 1,000 GWh annually.

Global carbon emissions from energy reached a record 37.4 billion tonnes in 2023.
